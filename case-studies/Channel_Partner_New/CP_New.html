02 / Problem

A fragmented workflow created repeated follow-ups.

Before the revamp, partners shared customer information through WhatsApp, Excel, and verbal communication. They often contacted multiple departments to establish lead ownership, check progress, and get updates.

Before

Disconnected partner experience

Customer information moved between partners, MIS, and pre-sales without a single, easily accessible view of the entire journey.

Bring onboarding, lead activity, site visits, project information, booking visibility, and commission-related information into a structured partner experience.

03 / User Research

Understanding different partner needs.

I gathered requirements through discussions with external and internal channel partners and considered the operational workflows involving MIS and pre-sales teams.

👤

External Channel Partners

Needed simple registration, customer lead tracking, visit scheduling, project details, booking status, and commission visibility.

🏢

Internal Channel Partners

Needed easier access to project knowledge, video tutorials, and learning incentives to support customer conversations.

✓

MIS Team

Responsible for verifying partner details and managing the approval dependencies associated with partner registration.

Key Research Insights

Lead visibility

One customer could be interested in multiple projects. Partners needed to track each lead without losing the customer context.

Project knowledge

Partners wanted project details, supporting documents, and video tutorials directly inside the portal.

Scheduling flexibility

Partners needed to schedule and reschedule visits when customer availability changed.

Post-booking transparency

Partners wanted visibility into booking confirmation and applicable brokerage or commission information.

04 / Information Architecture

One platform, multiple partner journeys.

External partners could register as individuals or companies, with separate paths for new and existing Sumadhura partners.

Individual Partner

New Individual CP Existing Individual CP

Registration captures the relevant personal information and routes the application through MIS verification.

Company Partner

New Company CP Existing Company CP

Registration captures company-related information with the appropriate partner onboarding flow.

Important Business Rule

While an external partner's MIS verification is pending, the partner can still submit customer leads. However, lead approval remains dependent on completion of partner verification.

06 / Design Challenge

Making a complex system feel simple.

The primary challenge was covering the complete partner journey without overwhelming users with complicated navigation or forcing them to search multiple locations for information.

Complex Registration

Support different partner categories while keeping the onboarding experience understandable.

Approval Dependencies

Allow useful activity during registration review without misrepresenting lead approval eligibility.

Multiple Customer Leads

Reduce unnecessary clicks when a single customer is associated with multiple project opportunities.

07 / Design Solutions

Designing for clarity, control, and visibility.

The solution focused on simplifying the most important partner workflows and presenting relevant information in a single connected experience.

08 / Testing & Iteration

Improving the flow through partner feedback.

After presenting the design to channel partners, I gathered feedback about the effort required to navigate customer and lead information.

Initial Flow

Too many navigation steps

Viewing several project leads belonging to the same customer required multiple interactions.

Refined Flow

More direct lead access

I revised the flow to reduce unnecessary clicks and make it easier to move between a customer's related leads.

09 / Outcomes

What changed for channel partners?

Feedback collected after implementation highlighted improvements in project visibility, access to learning resources, and the convenience of having information in one platform.

Project Visibility

Partners reported being able to view Sumadhura projects and access project-specific details more easily.

Self-Service Learning

Video tutorials helped partners understand projects when written information was not sufficient.

Connected Experience

Partners appreciated finding project information and learning resources within the same software.

Partner Feedback — Paraphrased
“I can now explore Sumadhura projects, access detailed information, watch tutorials when I need more clarity, and gain knowledge while earning reward points — all within one platform.”

10 / Key Learnings

What this project taught me.

Design for real workflows

Understanding how partners coordinate with MIS and pre-sales was essential to structuring the experience.

Make system states visible

Clear registration and approval messaging helps users understand what they can do next.

Reduce interaction effort

User feedback helped identify unnecessary navigation steps in the customer lead journey.
