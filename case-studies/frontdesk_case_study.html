01 The context

One entry point.
Many moving parts.

FrontDesk brings visitor registration, workplace entry tracking, courier handling, and management reporting into one structured operational experience.

As the end-to-end designer, I worked from requirement gathering and workflow planning through UI design, engineering collaboration, implementation support, and receptionist training.

01↗

Receptionists

Register different visit types, find employee information, manage check-ins, and retrieve visit records.

02◎

Visitors

Share essential information through assisted registration or a QR-led mobile flow, then receive an access pass.

03▥

Management

Review visitor activity and operational trends across locations, departments, and reporting periods.

02 Defining the challenge

Not just the moment
someone walks in.

The experience had to work across visitor categories, office teams, connected systems, and management needs—not just collect a visitor’s details.

A

Different entry scenarios

Customers, couriers, interview candidates, channel partners, and VIPs each bring different information and handling needs.

B

Reliable information

Registration depended partly on Salesforce and greythr data, where available fields did not always match the initial designs.

C

Visibility after check-in

Receptionists needed actionable visit records; management needed summarized, filterable, and shareable views across sites.

“

How might we make workplace check-in easier to operate while keeping visitor information, approval activity, and reporting organized across locations?

08 Working through constraints

The design had to work
with real systems.

Technical feasibility became a key consideration during handoff. Engineering raised implementation and timing constraints, while integration stakeholders clarified what Salesforce and Great HR data could provide.

01 / Feasibility

Design for what can ship

Revisited parts of the interface when proposed behavior could not be delivered within the available implementation window.

02 / Salesforce

Account for data gaps

Adapted fields and flows to available Salesforce data, including gaps in project-related information.

03 / Great HR

Align with API availability

Worked with HR and technical stakeholders to revise employee-related fields around available API data.

↗ The first design was a hypothesis, not a fixed specification. I adjusted scope and data capture so the experience remained implementable.
